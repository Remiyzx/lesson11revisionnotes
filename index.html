Lesson 11 Key Sentences & Overview

Lesson 11 Key Sentences Pg 180
Interactive Mode: Click on any sentence to reveal its English translation!
1. Qǐngwèn, xiànzài jǐ diǎn?
(May I ask, what time is it now?)
2. Wǒ huì shuō yìdiǎnr Hànyǔ.
(I can speak a little Chinese.)
3. Nǐmen jǐ diǎn shàngkè?
(What time do you have class?)
4. Xiànzài chà wǔ fēn bā diǎn, nín hái yǒu wǔ fēnzhōng.
(Now it is five minutes to eight, you still have five minutes.)
5. Tā wèi shénme bù néng lái shàngkè?
(Why can't he come to class?)
6. Tā zuótiān xiàwǔ qù péngyou jiā wánr.
(Yesterday afternoon he went to a friend's home to hang out.)
7. Tā yīnggāi lái shàngkè.
(He should come to class.)
8. Wǒ néng bu néng wèn nín yí ge wèntí?
(May I ask you a question?)
Grammar Points Quick Summary
• Clock Time & Quarters: chà uses subtraction (count backward to next hour) vs kè uses addition (count forward). diǎnzhōng is for exact hours only, fēnzhōng is for duration!
• Modals Comparison: huì (learned skill) vs néng (physical/conditional ability) vs kěyǐ (permission/prohibition) vs yīnggāi (obligation).
• Double Objects Verbs: jiāo (teach) and wèn (ask) take Subject + Verb + Person + Thing.
• Serial Verbs for Purpose: Subject + V1 + Place + V2 + Action (e.g., qù shāngchǎng mǎi dōngxi).
• Adverb hái ("still/yet"): Used with méi or bù to show something hasn't happened yet (e.g., tā hái méiyǒu qǐchuáng).

Grammar Rules & Deep Dive

1. "chà" vs "kè" Calculation Logic Pg 185
chà SUBTRACT ➖
  • Meaning: short of / before
  • Logic: Count backward from the next full hour
  • Clock Time: 7:45
    chà yí kè bā diǎn
    = 8:00 - 15 minutes
    ➔ a quarter to eight
kè ADD ➕
  • Meaning: quarter (15 minutes)
  • • yí kè = 15 mins | • liǎng kè = 30 mins
  • • sān kè = 45 mins
  • Clock Time: 8:15
    bā diǎn yí kè
    = 8:00 + 15 minutes
    ➔ a quarter past eight
2. Modals Comparison: kěyǐ, huì, and néng Pg 185-186
kěyǐ (boleh) huì (pandai) néng (dapat)
Permission / Appropriateness
  • Asking or giving permission; allowed.
  • When conditions make it suitable.
• Wǒ kěyǐ qù yóuyǒng ma?
(Can I go swimming?)

• Zhèr bù kěyǐ yóuyǒng.
(You can't swim here. Forbidden!)
Allowed or not
 Learned Skill / Ability
  • Talking about skills you have learned.
  • No ability because never learned.
• Wǒ huì yóuyǒng.
(I can swim. I learned it.)

• Wǒ bú huì yóuyǒng.
(I can't swim. I never learned it.)
Learned or not
 Physical / Conditional Ability
  • Talking about strength or conditions that allow something.
  • When external factors prevent action.
• Wǒ néng yóu shí gōnglǐ.
(I can swim 10km. I'm strong enough.)

• Wǒ bù néng yóuyǒng.
(I can't swim today. I'm sick.)
Physically possible or not

Core Dialogue Breakdown & Key Notes

Text 1 Breakdown: Asking Time & Expressing Ability Pg 174-175
Key Dialogue Highlights (Lines 1–19):
• Shīfu, wǒ qù Yǔyán Xuéyuàn. Qǐngwèn xiànzài jǐ diǎn? (Addressing taxi driver as "Shīfu"; polite time inquiry "qǐngwèn xiànzài jǐ diǎn?")
• Chà yí kè bā diǎn. Nín huì shuō Hànyǔ a! (7:45 AM = chà yí kè bā diǎn; "huì" indicates acquired skill)
• Wǒ huì shuō yìdiǎnr Hànyǔ. Wǒ shì xuésheng, xiànzài huí xuéyuàn shàngkè. (Serial verb for purpose: huí xuéyuàn shàngkè)
• Bā diǎn shàngkè. Shīfu, wǒmen bā diǎn néng dào ma? ➔ Néng dào. ("néng" expresses conditional/physical ability to arrive)
• Nǎli, wǒ de Hànyǔ bú tài hǎo. (Modest denial "nǎli" to praise)
• Xiànzài chà wǔ fēn bā diǎn, nín hái yǒu wǔ fēnzhōng. (7:55 AM; "fēnzhōng" used for remaining duration)
Text 2 Breakdown: Routine & Requesting Permission Pg 177-178
Key Dialogue Highlights (Lines 1–7):
• Chén lǎoshī, Mǎ Dàwéi jīntiān bù néng lái shàngkè. (Negation of conditional ability: "bù néng lái shàngkè")
• Tā wèi shénme bù néng lái shàngkè? (Question word "wèi shénme" for underlying reasons)
• Zuótiān shì xīngqīrì... tā wǎnshang shíyī diǎn bàn huí xuéyuàn, shí'èr diǎn xiě Hànzì, liǎng diǎnzhōng shuìjiào. Xiànzài tā hái méiyǒu qǐchuáng. (Chronological timeline of daily activities; "hái méiyǒu qǐchuáng" still hasn't gotten up)
• Lǎoshī, wǒ néng bu néng wèn nín yí ge wèntí? ➔ Kěyǐ. (Affirmative-negative "néng bu néng"; "kěyǐ" gives permission)

Xīngqītiān Lín Nà dào yí ge Yīngguó péngyou jiā wánr. Xīngqīyī bā diǎn tā yǒu kè, qī diǎn sān kè tā dǎdī huí xuéyuàn shàngkè. Nàge sījī jīnnián wǔshí'èr suì, tā yǒu yí ge sūnnür, jīnnián liù suì. Dīng Lìbō shuō Mǎ Dàwéi xiànzài hái méiyǒu qǐchuáng, tā bù néng lái shàngkè.
English Translation:

On Sunday, Lin Na went to a British friend's home to hang out. On Monday at 8:00 AM she has class, so at 7:45 AM she took a taxi back to the institute to attend class. That driver is 52 years old this year, and he has a granddaughter who is 6 years old this year. He said Lin Na's Chinese is very good. He also likes foreign languages, and is now learning English from his granddaughter. They arrived at the institute at 7:55 AM.

Ma Dawei was very busy on Sunday. In the morning he went to the mall to buy things, in the afternoon he went to a friend's home to hang out, and returned to the institute very late at night. He wrote Chinese characters at 12:00 AM, and went to sleep at 2:00 AM. Class started at 8:00 AM on Monday, but at 8:10 AM he still hadn't gotten up. Teacher Chen was very unhappy, and asked Ding Libo: "Where is Ma Dawei?" Ding Libo said Ma Dawei still hasn't gotten up now, so he cannot come to class.
